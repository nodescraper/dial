# Dial app icon

A glossy bakelite record with an iridescent pink, violet and blue rim and a pearl label, on graphite.
The PNGs here are the 1024 px masters (Light, Dark, Tinted). They are drawn in code, not in a design tool:
the renderer is a small SwiftUI script (RecordArt, FullIcon, MacIcon) that also makes the Mac sizes, the Android adaptive
icon layers and the TV banner.
